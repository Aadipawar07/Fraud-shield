```
**Detects:** Authority + Urgency + Phishing URL + Fear tactics

### **Scenario 2: Multilingual Scam** 🌍
```
बधाई! लॉटरी में ₹50 लाख मिले। Claim के लिए details भेजें। Only 24 hours left!
```
**Detects:** Hinglish processing + Prize scam + Information harvesting

### **Scenario 3: Investment Fraud** 💰
```
Hello! I am Rajesh, your HDFC manager. Exclusive 30% returns. Pre-approved. Limited to 10 customers.
```
**Detects:** Authority impersonation + Trust building + Scarcity tactics

INCOME TAX: Tax evasion case filed. Arrest warrant issued. Pay ₹45,000 in 4 hours to avoid arrest.
```
**Detects:** Government impersonation + Fear tactics + Payment demands
