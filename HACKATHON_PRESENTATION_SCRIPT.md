*A: Our multi-layered approach achieves 95%+ accuracy by combining AI analysis, pattern matching, URL intelligence, and social engineering detection. The adaptive learning system continuously improves accuracy based on user feedback.*

*A: Our personalized alert system learns from user feedback and adjusts sensitivity. Users with high false positive rates get more conservative alerts, while maintaining protection.*
